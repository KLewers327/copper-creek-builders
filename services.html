How We Work

Our 3-Step Building Process

A clear, predictable workflow from our first handshake to key handoff.

01. Discovery & Estimate

We meet on-site or review your plans to align on scope, target budget, and realistic timelines before breaking ground.

02. Pre-Construction

We finalize engineering, pull municipal permits, lock in trade schedules, and guide your material selections.

03. Build & Walkthrough

Dedicated on-site supervision with weekly progress updates, culminating in a thorough final walkthrough and 5-year warranty.
